Started with a light organ and PAR38 lamps in homemade plywood boxes in the 90s. Now it's interactive light installations built on DMX and sensor‑driven control.

Based in Norrköping, Sweden, gobo.ws is a freelance practice focused on custom integrations between DMX and modern lighting systems, along with Node‑RED flows for responsive control.

  • Awarded a 2026 grant from the Bertil & Britt Svenssons Foundation for Lighting Technology for Stad i puls , a data‑driven light visualization project
  • Built and installed an interactive light artwork for Vinterljus 2025 in Linköping
